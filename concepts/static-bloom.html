Why this direction works

A little rebellious.
Very easy to get.

Poster-scale headlines, hard grid lines, and punchy acid accents borrow the confidence of DIY gig flyers without making the interface hard to use. The visual system feels independent and unmistakable, while the clear section labels, short copy, and strong contrast keep the story scannable. It is the most disruptive option for a brand that wants to challenge how music communities work.
